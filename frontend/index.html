About the Data & Privacy

AgriTrace operates with full data transparency. Here is how reference datasets are used and how your data is handled.

What Leaves This Server?

  • When AI is in offline/rule-based mode: Zero data leaves this server. All reasoning runs locally.
  • When Google Gemini is active: Only anonymized field details (crop, soil type, readings) and problem text are transmitted. Your name, username, and exact GPS coordinates are NEVER sent.
  • When Supabase cloud backup is active: Encrypted backup occurs only upon logout.
  • Your Rights: You can delete your account and all associated farm data at any time.

Reference Soil Health Records (KVK Bareilly)

Compiled for academic and research use. All personal identifying information (farmer names, villages, Aadhaar, and phone numbers) is strictly redacted for privacy. High-efficiency 480px WebP thumbnails are loaded to conserve your mobile data.
